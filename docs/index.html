About

Built on structure,
driven by results.

HMPX Studio is a one-person digital studio focused on helping small businesses get online with clean, reliable, and purposeful websites.

HMPX Studio was founded by Michael Mokubung with a simple belief: small businesses deserve websites that are properly built — not cookie-cutter templates that look the same as everyone else.

Based in South Africa, the studio specialises in handcrafted HTML/CSS websites with an emphasis on clean structure, clarity of design, and technical reliability. Every project is handled personally — from the first conversation to deployment and beyond.

The mission is straightforward: help businesses establish a trustworthy online presence that supports long-term growth. No fluff. No bloat. Just work that performs.
